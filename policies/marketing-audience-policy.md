# Marketing Audience Policy

## MARKETING-01 — Permission to contact
Only students with an active marketing-consent flag may be included in promotional audiences. Consent is a mandatory system filter and cannot be disabled by a marketing requester or by the language model.

## CAMPAIGN-02 — Declared campaign purpose
Every audience request must identify a specific campaign purpose or target course. Open-ended requests for contact data are not valid campaign audience requests.

## CAMPAIGN-04 — Large audience approval
Audiences larger than 5,000 eligible recipients require approval by the manager identified in the original request before they may be released to a marketing platform.

## CAMPAIGN-07 — Existing customer exclusion
Students already enrolled in the course being promoted must be excluded from that course's acquisition campaign audience unless a separate retention or upsell policy explicitly applies.
