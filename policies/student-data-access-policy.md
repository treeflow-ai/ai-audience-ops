# Student Data Access Policy

## DATA-03 — No raw email export for marketing segmentation
Marketing users may request governed audience segments but may not directly export raw student email addresses, student IDs, or bulk personally identifiable information. Eligible records must be handed to an approved downstream marketing integration inside the controlled workflow.

## DATA-05 — Data minimization
The audience review screen should display aggregate counts, criteria, policy results, and audit metadata. Personally identifiable information is not required to approve a segment and should not be shown by default.

## DATA-08 — Purpose limitation
Student data may only be processed for the declared audience purpose. Reuse of a generated audience for a materially different campaign requires a new request and policy evaluation.
