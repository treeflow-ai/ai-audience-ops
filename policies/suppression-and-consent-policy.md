# Suppression and Consent Policy

## CONSENT-01 — Suppression overrides eligibility
Students on an email suppression list must be excluded even if other audience criteria match and even if a historical marketing-consent flag exists.

## CONSENT-02 — Active account requirement
Closed, deleted, or inactive student accounts are excluded from promotional audience creation.

## CONSENT-03 — Downstream permission preservation
Marketing integrations must preserve unsubscribe and permission-to-send states. A sync must never be used to re-subscribe a contact who has opted out in the downstream marketing system.
